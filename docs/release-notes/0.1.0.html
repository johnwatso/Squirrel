Squirrel 0.1.0 · 6 November 2026

Squirrel’s first release is here! 🐿️

Your Mac can finally see the UPS on your network. Squirrel connects to a Network UPS Tools (NUT) server and shares your UPS with macOS as a real power source, so the Mac’s own UPS shutdown settings can take over when the power goes out.

Meet Squirrel

  • A quick start. Find NUT servers on your network, choose your UPS, and get connected with the setup assistant.
  • Your power, at a glance. See battery charge, estimated runtime, load, and power in a native Mac window and the menu bar.
  • Built-in macOS power protection. Share one UPS with macOS and use System Settings → Energy → UPS Options to set shutdown thresholds. No sudo, sudoers edits, or helper required.
  • A clearer picture of outages. Explore UPS history, compare UPS and Mac temperatures, and review events such as power failures, low battery, and connection changes.
  • Make it your own. Monitor multiple UPSes, enable notifications, keep your Mac awake during an outage, and manage macOS power schedules.
  • Ready for your workflows. Automate with Shortcuts actions and the bundled squirrelctl command.

Get started

Download Squirrel-0.1.0.zip, unzip it, move Squirrel to Applications, and follow the setup assistant. You’ll need macOS 13 or later and access to a NUT server. Allow Local Network access when prompted.

Keep Squirrel running to share your UPS with macOS, and test an outage before relying on automatic shutdown. Use your NUT connection on a trusted network.

This is the beginning of Squirrel. Thanks for trying the first release—we’d love to hear how it works with your UPS and Mac!
